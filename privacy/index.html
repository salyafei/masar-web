TheNibras Technologies (Dubai, United Arab Emirates) makes apps, websites and software, including the Masar app and thenibras.com. This page explains how we handle your data. 1. Masar app

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

Masar is a free learning app for UAE real estate, for learners and for hiring partners (companies). It has no payments, no in-app purchases, no prices and no ads. Hiring partners are billed separately under their own agreement with TheNibras, outside the app. Masar is for people aged 13 and over.

What we collect, and why

  • Sign-in details: your mobile number or email address. We use it only to send you a one-time code and to identify your account. You type it on the sign-in screen. Codes sent by SMS go through Twilio Verify (United States), which uses your number only to deliver the code. Codes sent by email go from noreply@thenibras.com through our Namecheap mail server (SMTP). There is no password and no social login.
  • Profile: display name, emirate, nationality, career interests, language, and your choice whether to be visible to hiring partners (the "visible to hiring partners" switch on your profile). You enter these yourself. We use them to run your account and personalise learning.
  • Nationality is sensitive. We treat your nationality as sensitive personal data in the UAE context. Other learners never see it. Hiring partners see it only if you opt in. We use it only for your profile and, if you opt in, for partners, and never for advertising or profiling.
  • Learning activity: answers, scores, XP, streaks, duel, season and exam results, certificates and badges. Created as you use the app. We use it to provide learning, rankings and certificates.
  • Exam integrity signals: during timed exams the app counts how often you leave the app and how often you copy, cut or paste, and reduces this to a result of clean, review or flagged. We use it only to keep exams and certificates fair. We do not use the camera or microphone, and we do not read what you copy. You can ask for a person to review a result (section 9).
  • Uploaded evidence files: written answers, links, friend-league names, and files you attach as project evidence (JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP or PDF). Files are kept in a private storage area that only your account can access (row-level security), and are visible to others only as far as you choose to share the submission.
  • Hiring partner accounts: company name, member profiles (name, phone number or email address, role), the access end date set by the Masar team, and the invites sent to learners.
  • Not collected: payment or card data, location, contacts, camera, microphone, push notification tokens, or advertising identifiers. Masar has no analytics, crash-reporting or diagnostic SDKs.

Opt-in partner visibility: who can see what

  • Other learners see your first name and initial, rank and weekly XP on leaderboards and in friend leagues you join.
  • Hiring partners approved by the Masar team can see exactly these five things about you: your name, emirate, nationality, ranking and exam integrity result (clean, review or flagged). They see them only if you turn on "visible to hiring partners", and nothing else. They see your phone number and email only after you accept their invite.
  • The public can see your skill passport only if you make it public.
  • We do not show your data to anyone else, except our service providers (section 6) and where the law requires.

Where your data is stored, and cross-border transfer

Masar's database and uploaded files are stored with Supabase in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1), so your data is transferred outside the UAE. SMS codes are handled by Twilio in the United States. We make these transfers under Articles 22 and 23 of the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (section 7).

Storage on your device

Masar sets no cookies and does not use sessionStorage or IndexedDB, and it has no tracking or ads. It uses local storage (localStorage on the web; private on-device storage on iOS) only for: your sign-in session tokens, so you stay signed in; your chosen language and a one-time reload marker used when switching to Arabic; your light or dark theme; and the progress of the sample quiz you can try before signing up, which is cleared when you sign in. Clearing your browser's site data, or signing out, removes this. App updates are delivered only through the App Store; Masar does not check for updates itself and sends no device or install identifier to any update service.

Withdraw consent, and delete your account

  • Turn off "visible to hiring partners" on your profile at any time. Partners then stop seeing your profile data.
  • Decline or ignore a partner's invite. Your contact details are not released unless you accept.
  • Make your skill passport private.
  • Delete your account in the app, immediately and completely: learners go to Profile, then Delete account. Partners go to Account, then Delete account. You confirm in two steps. Your uploaded files and sign-in record are deleted at once, and this removes all your rows of data. If you own a friend league that others have joined, it passes to another member, and other records that referred to you are cleared of your identity. Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's routine system backups for a limited period, as explained in section 8.
  • You can also email support@thenibras.com from your registered email or phone contact and we will delete your account for you.

  • الملف الشخصي: الاسم المعروض والإمارة والجنسية والاهتمامات المهنية واللغة، وخيارك في الظهور لشركاء التوظيف (مفتاح «ظاهر لشركاء التوظيف» في ملفك الشخصي). تدخل هذه البيانات بنفسك، ونستخدمها لتشغيل حسابك وتخصيص التعلم.

  • نشاط التعلم: الإجابات والنتائج ونقاط الخبرة والسلاسل ونتائج المبارزات والمواسم والامتحانات والشهادات والشارات. تُنشأ أثناء استخدامك التطبيق، ونستخدمها لتقديم التعلم والترتيب والشهادات.
  • مؤشرات نزاهة الامتحان: في الامتحانات الموقوتة يحصي التطبيق عدد مرات مغادرتك له وعدد مرات النسخ والقص واللصق، ثم يختصر ذلك في نتيجة: سليم أو للمراجعة أو مُعلَّم. نستخدمها فقط لضمان عدالة الامتحانات والشهادات. لا نستخدم الكاميرا ولا الميكروفون، ولا نقرأ ما تنسخه. ويمكنك طلب أن يراجع شخص النتيجة (القسم 9).
  • ملفات الأدلة المرفوعة: الإجابات المكتوبة والروابط وأسماء دوريات الأصدقاء والملفات التي ترفقها دليلاً على مشروعك (JPEG أو PNG أو HEIC أو WebP أو PDF). تُحفظ الملفات في مساحة تخزين خاصة لا يصل إليها إلا حسابك (أمان على مستوى الصفوف)، ولا يراها غيرك إلا بالقدر الذي تختار مشاركته من المشاركة.
  • حسابات شركاء التوظيف: اسم الشركة وملفات الأعضاء (الاسم ورقم الجوال أو البريد الإلكتروني والدور) وتاريخ انتهاء الوصول الذي يحدده فريق مسار والدعوات المرسلة إلى المتعلمين.

  • يمكنك سحب هذا الإذن في أي وقت من داخل المنتج أو بمراسلة support@thenibras.com.

7. النقل الدولي للبيانات

نبراس شركة مقرها الإمارات، لكن قاعدة بيانات مسار موجودة في الهند، وبعض المزودين (Twilio وStripe) يعالجون البيانات في الولايات المتحدة أو غيرها. وبموجب قانون حماية البيانات الشخصية في دولة الإمارات (المرسوم بقانون اتحادي رقم 45 لسنة 2021، «القانون»، المادتان 22 و23) لا ننقل البيانات الشخصية خارج الدولة إلا إلى دول أو مزودين يوفرون حماية مناسبة، وبموجب عقود تلزمهم بتطبيق ضمانات القانون، أو عند موافقتك الصريحة أو عندما يكون النقل لازماً لتقديم الخدمة التي طلبتها. وبتسجيل دخولك واستخدامك مسار فإنك تطلب منا تقديمه، وهذا يستلزم هذا النقل.
