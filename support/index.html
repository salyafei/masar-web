Masar Support

Questions, bug reports or data requests: email support@thenibras.com. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

  • I did not get my code. Check the number (UAE mobile, nine digits starting with 5) or sign in with your email instead, then wait a minute and tap Resend.
  • How do I delete my account? Profile, then Delete account. Everything is erased immediately.
  • Who can see my profile? Only you, unless you turn on visibility to hiring partners. See the Privacy Policy.
